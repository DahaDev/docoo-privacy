Troubleshooting

The camera won't open

Check that Docoo has camera access in your iPhone settings. You can also import an existing photo from the app.

The scan is blurry or the edges are wrong

Put the page on a flat, contrasting surface in even light. Hold your phone steady, then retake the scan or adjust the page edges in the review screen.

The PDF won't export or share

Check your available device storage, reopen the saved document, and try exporting again. If sharing fails in another app, try saving the PDF to Files first.

Premium hasn't appeared

Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription, then select Restore Purchases in Docoo. You can check whether the subscription is active under Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Contact support

Email info@artofdaha.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Docoo version, and what happened. Leave out sensitive documents and payment details.
